Practice — 6 questions

Read → pause → commit to an answer → then reveal.
3 on one stimulus (GreenBox)· 3 standalone
Stimulus

Scenario

Elena Reyes owns GreenBox, a meal-prep delivery service.

  • City council debating a tax on sugary packaged foods
  • A fitness lifestyle trend is growing; gym memberships rising 3 straight years
  • New state law requires every food delivery business to hold a certified food-handler license within one year

Elena is deciding whether to expand into two nearby towns.

Practice
Q1Classify the fitness trend
AEconomic
BSocial
CTechnological
DPolitical
Answer B

Why

Social factors include lifestyle trends. A fitness trend changing what people eat = social.

A — gym memberships involve spending, but economic factors describe the condition of the economy itself.
Master ruleClassify by the source. A change in what people choose to value is social, even when it costs money.
Practice
Q2The paired-concept swap

The proposed tax on sugary foods and the food-handler license requirement are best classified as:

ALegal, then political
BPolitical, then environmental
CEconomic, then legal
DPolitical, then legal
Answer D

Why

Proposed tax = policy choice → political. A specific law requiring a license → legal.

A — swaps the pair. C — a tax affects money, but its source is a government policy decision.
Practice
Q3How to use the framework
AAdopt only after choosing which town to enter
BWeigh only factors that favor expansion
CIdentify relevant factors and assess how each affects attractiveness and risks
DTreat all six factors as equally important
Answer C

Why

Identify factors relevant to the specific product and market, then assess attractiveness and risk.

Warning"Treat every factor as equally weighted" sounds thorough — reads as a wrong answer.

C invents a social factor — table says nothing about values. D uses "guarantee."
Practice
Q5Solar-panel subsidies

A government offers large subsidies to businesses that install solar panels. Best describes this?

ALegal factor — subsidies are written into law
BEnvironmental factor — solar panels affect the environment
CPolitical factor that incentivizes a specific business activity
DTechnological factor — solar panels are technology
Answer C

Why

Subsidies are policy tools → political.

B — classify by source, not subject matter. The subsidy concerns solar panels, but the force is a government policy choice.
Practice
Q6Fiber internet reaches rural areas

A homework-help app operates where most households have high-speed internet. A new fiber network brings fast internet to rural areas for the first time. Best describes the effect?

ARaises a barrier to entry that protects the app from rivals
BA technological factor changed, expanding the app's viable market
CConverts the app's service from a want into a need
DA social factor — more people can now join online communities
Answer B

Why

Internet access = technological factor. When it spreads, the viable market grows.

A — borrows Topic 1.2 vocabulary. Wider internet access opens the market — the opposite of a barrier.
