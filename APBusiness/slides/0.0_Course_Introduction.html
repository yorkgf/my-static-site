Course Overview

What is this course?

The Big Picture An introductory, college-level course in business + personal finance.
  • College equivalent: one semester of intro-to-business
  • Prerequisites: none — just willingness to work individually and in teams
  • A Career Kickstart AP course — built with colleges and industry employers
  • Built straight from the official Course and Exam Description (CED)

You'll explore the business disciplines of entrepreneurship, marketing, finance, accounting, and management — plus personal finance.

Course Overview

What makes it different?

Career Kickstart

Developed with industry leaders, not just colleges → skills for real careers.

Work-based learning

You act like a founder — product development, customer research, marketing, financial documents.

Two projects, not just tests

The Business Canvas Project and the Financial Advisor Project.

Personal finance woven throughout

Not a separate afterthought — it runs through the whole course.

The Projects

Business Canvas Project

Assessed via: Exam-Day Validation FRQ

Financial Advisor Project

Unit 5. Recommend a financial plan to a fictional household.

Assessed via: Coursework

About the canvasA modified Business Model Canvas (6 components: Organization, Operations, Customer, Product, Market, Finance/Accounting). Template provided (CED + AP Classroom) — format is flexible.

Deep-dive

FRQ 1 — Business Canvas Validation

15% · 25 minutes Skills 1, 2, 4

On exam day you respond in writing about your own Business Canvas Project:

  1. Pitch your product idea
  2. Explain how you used hypothesis testing to inform a decision
  3. Explain why a common entrepreneurial challenge may threaten your product's viability
ImportantThe project itself isn't submitted — this FRQ is how your project work is scored. Keep your notebook/test results all year.

Mindset

How to succeed

  • Learn the definitions cold — then learn the traps (each topic's practice set lists them)
  • Follow the running story — it's the connective tissue across the whole course
  • Keep your Business Canvas alive all year — FRQ 1 depends on it
  • Match the method to the question — most wrong answers are right words in the wrong place
  • Do every practice set — the exam repeats the same trap patterns
